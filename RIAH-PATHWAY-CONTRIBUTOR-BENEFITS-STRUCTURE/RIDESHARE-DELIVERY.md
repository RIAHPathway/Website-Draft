## I. 👑 SHARED ELIGIBILITY

Participants must maintain an identifiable profile; complete required training; use approved RIAH Pathway branding and current materials; accurately represent programs, products, services and pathways; obtain required permissions; submit evidence for point-bearing activity; protect participant information; never fabricate activity or conversions; and receive verification before points become permanent.

## II. 🧮 SHARED BENEFIT FORMULA

**100 verified points = 1% in one selected eligible benefit ledger. Education, Experiential and Products are separate ledgers; each is capped at 2,500 points = 25%.**

## VIII. 🛡️ VERIFICATION & ROLE-SPECIFIC RESTRICTIONS

Rideshare materials must comply with applicable platform and vehicle requirements, must not interfere with passenger safety and must not create unwanted pressure on passengers.

Delivery marketing materials must not be placed inside customer food, packages, mailboxes, private property or delivery orders unless specifically permitted.

No points are awarded for fake, duplicate or self-referrals; fabricated activity; fraudulent transactions; QR manipulation; spam; plagiarism; unauthorized materials; misleading claims; pressure-based marketing; abandoned or rejected work; or restricted / confidential information.
