## I. 🤝 PARTNER & PARTNER EMPLOYEE STRUCTURE

RIAH Pathway partners may qualify for tuition and product benefits through an approved partnership relationship and applicable written terms.

Partner Employees are verified eligible employees, members, participants or approved beneficiaries associated with an active approved RIAH Pathway partner. Partner Employee Ambassador activity may include partner orientation, partner events, booths / tables, presentations, joint outreach, approved partner resources, joint initiatives, referrals and verified conversions.

Partner Affiliates are separate from Partner Employees. Affiliate referral and attribution rules are maintained in [AFFILIATES.md](./AFFILIATES.md).

## II. 👑 ELIGIBILITY

1. Be associated with an active approved RIAH Pathway partner.
2. Meet the applicable written partnership requirements.
3. Have partner affiliation and participant eligibility verified.
4. Be an eligible employee, member, participant or approved beneficiary when using Partner Employee benefits.
5. Maintain eligibility during the applicable benefit period.
6. Follow admissions, product, program and partnership requirements.
7. Do not transfer benefits to unauthorized individuals.
8. Complete re-verification when the partnership relationship or eligibility changes.
9. Use approved RIAH Pathway materials and accurate program information.
10. Submit evidence for point-bearing activity and receive verification before points become permanent.

## III. 🧮 BENEFIT STRUCTURE

| Benefit | Structure |
|---|---|
| Partner / Partner Employee Ambassador Benefit | **Up to 25%** |
| Product Benefit | **Up to 25%** |
| Education / Experiential Completion | **10% guaranteed when otherwise eligible and completed** |
| Additional Tuition Reimbursement Milestones | **Up to 15% additional** |
| Education / Experiential Maximum | **Up to 50% total** |
| Multiple Ambassador Categories | **Do not stack beyond the applicable 25% Ambassador component** |

Partner benefits remain subject to the applicable written partnership classification and eligibility period. Partner activity points do not override written partnership terms.

## IV. ⭐ PARTNER EMPLOYEE POINT TIERS

| Approved Points | Ambassador Benefit |
|---:|---:|
| 100 | 1% |
| 500 | 5% |
| 1,000 | 10% |
| 1,500 | 15% |
| 2,000 | 20% |
| 2,500 | **25% MAX** |

Education, Experiential and Product benefit points are maintained in separate ledgers, each capped at **2,500 points = 25%**.

## VI. 🔗 CONVERSION MILESTONES

| Stage | Point Treatment |
|---|---|
| Qualified attributable referral | 10 points when applicable |
| Webinar / Information Session Attendance | +15 points when applicable |
| Completed Application | +25 points when applicable |
| Paying Education Pathway student conversion | Conversion recorded |
| Paying Experiential Pathway student conversion | Conversion recorded |
| Converted student reaches 50% program completion | **50 points** |

A paying student conversion does **not** automatically create a 25% benefit.

## VII. 🎪 SHARED AMBASSADOR ACTIVITIES

Partner and Partner Employee Ambassadors may also participate in approved community events, career / education events, workshops, webinars, booths, information tables, outreach campaigns and organizational introductions when applicable.

| Event Contribution | Points |
|---|---:|
| Attend and support approved event | 25 |
| Staff approved booth or table | 50 |
| Present approved session | 50 |
| Coordinate event participation | 75 |
| Organize approved RIAH workshop | 75 |
| Lead major approved event | 100 |
| Coordinate major multi-partner event | 150–200 |

Multiple awards require genuinely separate verified responsibilities.

## VIII. 📦 AMBASSADOR MATERIALS & ATTRIBUTION

Eligible non-team Ambassadors may use approved physical and digital materials, assigned referral links and individually attributable QR codes where applicable. Vehicle materials apply only where appropriate and permitted.

The personalized attribution route may connect people to RIAH Pathway Education Pathways, Experiential Pathways, Certification Review Programs, Bar Review Programs, Products and Services.

## IX. 🛡️ VERIFICATION & NON-STACKING

Verification may include active partner status, written partnership terms, employment / member / participant eligibility, event records, approved resources, referral / QR attribution, enrollment or product attribution, RIAH review and re-verification when affiliation changes.

Expired, transferred, duplicate, fabricated, unauthorized or unverifiable claims receive no points or benefit. One underlying activity normally receives one primary award unless separate point-bearing responsibilities or deliverables are independently verified.

## X. 📋 PARTNER / PARTNER EMPLOYEE RECORD

Record Participant, Participant ID, Partner Organization, Partner Classification, Eligibility Basis, Verification Date, Activity ID, Activity, Attribution, Points, Total Points, Applicable Tuition Benefit, Applicable Product Benefit, Written Agreement Reference, Approved By, Re-verification Date, Next Milestone and Status.
