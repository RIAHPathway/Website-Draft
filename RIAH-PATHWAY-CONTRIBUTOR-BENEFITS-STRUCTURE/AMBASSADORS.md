## X. 🧮 SHARED BENEFIT & ROUTING RULES

For eligible **non-team** participants, Education, Experiential and Products remain separate benefit categories.

| Benefit Category | Base / Maximum |
|---|---:|
| Education Completion | **10% guaranteed after eligible completion / graduation** |
| Experiential Completion | **10% guaranteed after eligible completion** |
| Education / Experiential Ambassador Component | **0%–25% additional** |
| Additional Tuition Reimbursement Milestones | **0%–15% additional** |
| Education / Experiential Maximum | **50% maximum** |
| Product Discount | **0%–25% per product-discount earning cycle** |

### Product Purchase Conversion

**Product purchase dollars = Product points. Product Discount % = Product points ÷ 100.**

| Verified Attributable Product Purchases | Product Points | Earned Product Discount |
|---:|---:|---:|
| $0 | 0 | 0% |
| $100 | 100 | 1% |
| $525 | 525 | 5.25% |
| $1,000 | 1,000 | 10% |
| $1,500 | 1,500 | 15% |
| $2,500 | 2,500 | **25% MAX** |

Products are non-refundable. The product-discount earning cycle caps at **$2,500 = 2,500 points = 25%**. The earned percentage remains available until used; when the 25% earning cycle is complete, the earning counter refreshes for a new cycle under the established rules.

### Shared referral / conversion treatment

| Stage | Point Treatment |
|---|---|
| Qualified Referral | 10 points where applicable |
| Webinar / Information Session Attendance | +15 points where applicable |
| Completed Application | +25 points where applicable |
| Paying Education Pathway student conversion | Conversion recorded |
| Paying Experiential Pathway student conversion | Conversion recorded |
| Converted student reaches 50% program completion | **50 points** |
| Converted student completes / graduates | **+50 points** |

Approved activities across categories may be combined when applicable, but **one underlying activity is credited once to one selected ledger unless genuinely separate responsibilities or deliverables are independently verified**. The applicable Ambassador component remains capped at 25%.
