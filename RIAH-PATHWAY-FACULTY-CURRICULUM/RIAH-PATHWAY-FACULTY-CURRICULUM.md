# I. 📚 PURPOSE AND FACULTY CURRICULUM STANDARD

RIAH Pathway's faculty curriculum structure defines how qualified academic faculty and applied professionals participate in the creation, development, review, revision, validation, and continuous improvement of curriculum across the ecosystem.

The Founder, CEO & Chairman, **Mariah Dominique Rucker**, establishes the overarching architecture, structure, pathway logic, ecosystem connections, and institutional design. The academic content model then brings qualified human faculty into the process so the curriculum is not dependent on one person, an AI system, a software vendor, or automated content generation.

The intended academic faculty structure includes **PhD-qualified faculty and adjunct faculty aligned to schools and applicable majors or academic disciplines**, working with experiential professionals and other qualified reviewers. At planned scale, the faculty model includes **14 PhD/Core faculty positions and 14 Adjunct faculty positions**, with assignments aligned to the approved academic structure and consolidated where related repository curriculum structures sit within the same faculty discipline.

| Curriculum Standard | RIAH Pathway Faculty Requirement |
|---|---|
| 👑 Institutional architecture | Founder, CEO & Chairman establishes the ecosystem architecture, curriculum structure, pathway framework, and institutional design |
| 🎓 Academic validation | PhD-qualified faculty provide discipline-level academic leadership, review, revision, and validation |
| 👤 Applied instruction | Adjunct faculty contribute subject-matter expertise, instructional experience, certification knowledge, and applied course development |
| 💼 Professional relevance | Experiential professionals connect academic learning to real work, professional standards, supervision, and applied outcomes |
| 👥 Human review | Required academic and professional decisions remain subject to qualified human review |
| 🔄 Continuous improvement | Curriculum is reviewed and revised as programs, standards, technologies, laws, certifications, employer needs, and accreditation expectations evolve |
| 🤖 Technology support | AI and software may assist faculty workflows but do not replace faculty authority, academic judgment, or professional supervision |
| 🔒 IP control | Proprietary curriculum and protected academic materials remain within controlled RIAH Pathway systems and access rules |

# IX. 🧪 ASSESSMENTS, CAPSTONES, AND ACADEMIC VALIDATION

The current curriculum structure requires proctored objective assessments, performance assessments, or both. Existing school-core structures specify **25-question objective assessments**, **task-based performance assessments**, an **80% minimum passing score**, and unlimited attempts where applicable.

Faculty are responsible for ensuring that assessment content actually measures the intended learning rather than merely repeating instructional text.

| Assessment Surface | Faculty Validation Standard |
|---|---|
| 📋 Objective assessments | Questions map to course outcomes, use appropriate difficulty, avoid ambiguity, and maintain controlled answer integrity |
| 🛠️ Performance assessments | Tasks measure applied competency through authentic or appropriately simulated work |
| ✅ Passing requirements | Standards are applied consistently and reviewed for academic defensibility |
| 🔄 Reattempts | Alternate or controlled assessment exposure protects integrity while supporting mastery |
| 🎯 Capstones | Cumulative work demonstrates integrated knowledge and applicable professional competence |
| 👥 Human panels | Applicable capstones receive multi-person human review under the established curriculum structure |
| 💼 Professional supervision | Applicable cumulative projects retain assigned industry-professional supervision |
| 🧾 Evidence | Rubrics, review records, assessment mappings, revisions, and outcomes are retained for quality and accreditation-readiness purposes |

# XI. 🏅 CERTIFICATION REVIEW CURRICULUM

Certification Review Courses are human-led academic and professional review products. Faculty and appropriately credentialed adjuncts are responsible for validating content against the applicable certification domain while maintaining clear separation between RIAH Pathway and third-party credential owners unless an official relationship exists.

| Certification Review Function | Human Faculty Requirement |
|---|---|
| Domain mapping | Map course content to the applicable published certification objectives or authorized framework |
| Academic explanation | Develop clear instruction that teaches the underlying knowledge rather than only test-taking patterns |
| Applied simulations | Build task-based practice appropriate to the certification domain |
| Objective assessments | Review question accuracy, difficulty, distractors, and domain coverage |
| Performance assessments | Validate task realism, scoring criteria, and expected competencies |
| Updates | Revise materials when certification objectives, technologies, standards, or exam versions change |
| Product review | Validate textbooks, workbooks, study guides, review guides, and practice materials |
