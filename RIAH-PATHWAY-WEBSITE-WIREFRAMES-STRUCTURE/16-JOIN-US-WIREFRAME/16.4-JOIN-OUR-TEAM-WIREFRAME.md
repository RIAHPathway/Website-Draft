**Founder & CEO — 1 • President — 1 • Vice President — 1 • Secretary — 1 • Treasurer / CPA — 1 • Trustee / Attorney at Law — 1**

**Institutional • Corporate • Foundation**

Oversees institutional, corporate, and foundation governance.

The President leads board governance; the Vice President supports governance operations; the Secretary maintains minutes, resolutions, records, and board documentation; the **Treasurer is a CPA** responsible for financial-governance oversight; and the **Trustee is an Attorney at Law** responsible for legal-governance oversight.
