# PRODUCT & SERVICE PROFESSIONAL CONTRACTOR

**Category:** External Team  
**Equity:** **0%**

### Work Model
**100% remote.**

### Description
Provide demand-based mentorship, coaching, live review, Certification Review, Bar Review, and other professional services sold through the RIAH Pathway product ecosystem.

### Requirements
- Certification Review contractors must hold the **actual certification being reviewed** and have **experience in the professional area connected to that certification**.
- Bar Review contractors must be **licensed attorneys** and have **experience as attorneys**.
- Mentorship, coaching, live review, and other professional-service contractors must have **experience in the professional area for the service they are delivering**.

### Compensation
Contract compensation based on product, service, demand, sales, and signed agreement.

### Equity & Contribution Pool
**No equity. No Contribution Pool requirement.**

### Technology
Contractors use technology meeting RIAH Pathway security and service requirements when accessing RIAH Pathway systems.

### Hiring Timeline
**Demand-based according to product sales, service utilization, student demand, and operational need.**
