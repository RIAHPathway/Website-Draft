# CHIEF INFORMATION SECURITY OFFICER

**Category:** Executive Leadership  
**Equity:** **1.68%**

### Work Model
**100% remote during the first year of operations**, including Executive Leadership and Board of Governance meetings.

After the first year of operations, the role remains primarily remote with required **quarterly in-person Executive Leadership and Board of Governance meetings in Cleveland, Ohio**.

### Description
The CISO leads cybersecurity, information security, infrastructure protection, network security, identity, access, risk, monitoring, incident response, and security architecture.

### Responsibilities
- Direct the Red, Blue, and Purple Team Specialists.
- Establish security architecture, controls, monitoring, and response.
- Protect applications, databases, networks, systems, endpoints, infrastructure, websites, and digital operations.
- Coordinate directly with the CTO and Backend Technology Team.
- Install and maintain required team security controls and security configurations.

### Requirements
- **Must have management experience in cybersecurity.**
- Must have experience leading or managing a cybersecurity team and cybersecurity operations.

### Preferences
Cybersecurity architecture, security operations, infrastructure security, risk, network security, incident response, or cybersecurity leadership experience.

### RIAH Pathway Standard Equity Terms for Equity-Bearing Positions
**Equity:** **1.68%**

**Vesting:** Four-year vesting with a one-year cliff. **25% after Year 1, 25% after Year 2, 25% after Year 3, and 25% after Year 4**, subject to continued participation, responsibilities, performance, contribution requirements, and signed agreements.

**Contribution Pool:**
- **Pre-Beta:** **$520.83 per month**
- **Beta:** **Varies as Core Experiential Professionals are hired**
- **Post-Accreditation / At Scale:** **$595.24 per month**
- **Due Date:** **15th of each month**

### Startup Technology Requirement
The team member purchases their required **work laptop and inexpensive work phone** during startup. The CTO and CISO ensure required software, security controls, applications, access controls, and technology tools are installed and configured.

The CTO maintains authorized remote administrative access for technology setup, maintenance, software installation, updates, troubleshooting, and support. The CISO maintains authorized security access needed to protect and maintain required cybersecurity controls.

Approved laptop and work-phone purchases are **reimbursed during the RIAH Pathway reimbursement period after student revenues begin**, according to the reimbursement process and required documentation.

### Executive Meeting Stipend
The stipend **does not begin during the first year of operations** while Board and Executive Leadership meetings remain remote.

After the first year, the role receives **$5,000 annually — $1,250 quarterly** for lodging, food, and transportation connected to required Cleveland meetings.
