# RIAH PATHWAY — EQUITY AND FOUR-YEAR VESTING TABLE

## EQUITY VESTING STRUCTURE

Equity-bearing positions **accrue equity monthly throughout each vesting year** and follow a **four-year vesting schedule with a one-year cliff**.

The equity shown for each year is the equity tranche that vests at the completion of that vesting year. The applicable **individual-role equity or combined dual-role equity is fully vested at the completion of Year 4**.

Where a dual role applies, the table separately identifies the **individual-role equity**, the **dual-role add-on equity**, and the **combined dual-role equity**.

## VESTING DETAIL

### Individual-Role Equity

**Vesting Individual Equity:** Individual-role equity accrues monthly throughout each vesting year and is subject to the one-year cliff. The applicable annual tranche vests at the completion of Year 1, Year 2, Year 3, and Year 4. The full individual-role equity is vested at the completion of Year 4.

### Dual-Role Equity

**Vesting Dual-Role Equity:** When a dual role is formally assigned, both the individual-role equity and the dual-role add-on equity accrue monthly throughout each vesting year. The combined annual tranche vests at the completion of Year 1, Year 2, Year 3, and Year 4. The full combined dual-role equity is vested at the completion of Year 4.

### One-Year Cliff

The first annual equity tranche remains subject to the **one-year cliff** and does not vest until the completion of Year 1.

### Full Vesting

At the completion of **Year 4**, the applicable total individual-role equity or combined dual-role equity is **fully vested**.
