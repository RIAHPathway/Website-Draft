### Certification Review / Bar Review Dual Role
PhD Academic Faculty may hold a **Certification Review dual role based on their school, major, discipline, certification, and assigned responsibilities**.

Certification Review faculty must:
- Hold the **actual certification being reviewed**.
- Have **experience in the professional area connected to that certification**.

School of Law PhD Academic Faculty performing Bar Review must:
- Hold an active attorney license.
- Have **experience as an attorney**.

Approved PhD dual-role equity reference:
- **Base PhD Academic Faculty Equity:** **0.1416%**
- **Approved Additional Dual-Role Equity:** **+0.1416%**
- **Total Designated Dual-Role Equity:** **0.2832%**

The additional dual-role allocation applies only when the additional equity-bearing responsibility is formally assigned.

### Requirements
- **Must hold a PhD or doctorate in the assigned major, discipline, or equivalent academic subject area.**

No separate professional-experience requirement applies to the standard PhD Academic Faculty role.

### Startup Technology Requirement
The team member purchases their required **work laptop and inexpensive work phone** during startup. The CTO and CISO ensure required software, security controls, applications, access controls, and technology tools are installed and configured.

The CTO maintains authorized remote administrative access for technology setup, maintenance, software installation, updates, troubleshooting, and support. The CISO maintains authorized security access needed to protect and maintain required cybersecurity controls.

Approved laptop and work-phone purchases are **reimbursed during the RIAH Pathway reimbursement period after student revenues begin**, according to the reimbursement process and required documentation.
