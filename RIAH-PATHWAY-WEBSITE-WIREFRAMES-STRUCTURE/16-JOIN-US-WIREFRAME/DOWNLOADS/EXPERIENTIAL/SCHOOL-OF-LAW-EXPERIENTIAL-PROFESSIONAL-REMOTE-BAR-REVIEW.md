# SCHOOL OF LAW EXPERIENTIAL PROFESSIONAL — REMOTE BAR REVIEW

**Core Positions:** 30  
**Individual Equity:** **0.0280%**

### Work Model
**100% remote.**

### Description
RIAH Pathway is seeking School of LAW Experiential Professionals serving as **Managers, Supervisors, or Reviewers** while supporting **Remote Bar Review**.

Applicants apply for the Manager, Supervisor, or Reviewer function that matches their professional experience.

### Experiential Role Requirements

**Manager**
- Must have **senior-level, manager-level, or higher professional experience**.

**Supervisor**
- Must have **manager-level or higher professional experience**.

**Reviewer**
- Must have **at least senior-level professional experience**.

### Remote Bar Review Dual Role
Professionals performing Remote Bar Review must:
- Be a **currently licensed attorney**.
- Maintain an active attorney license while performing the Bar Review role.
- Have **experience as an attorney**.

The attorney does not have to be licensed in every state included within the assigned RIAH Pathway Bar Review region.

Regional assignments: **North, South, East, West**.

### Equity
- Experiential role: **0.0140%**
- Remote Bar Review dual role: **+0.0140%**
- **Total individual equity: 0.0280%**

### RIAH Pathway Standard Equity Terms for Equity-Bearing Positions
**Vesting:** Four years, one-year cliff, **25% annually**.

**Contribution:** Pre-Beta **$520.83 monthly**; Beta **varies as Experiential Professionals are hired**; Post-Accreditation / At Scale **$595.24 monthly**; due **15th of each month**.

### Startup Technology Requirement
The team member purchases their required **work laptop and inexpensive work phone** during startup. The CTO and CISO ensure required software, security controls, applications, access controls, and technology tools are installed and configured.

The CTO maintains authorized remote administrative access for technology setup, maintenance, software installation, updates, troubleshooting, and support. The CISO maintains authorized security access needed to protect and maintain required cybersecurity controls.

Approved laptop and work-phone purchases are **reimbursed during the RIAH Pathway reimbursement period after student revenues begin**, according to the reimbursement process and required documentation.

Deadline **November 30, 2026** → Interviews **December 1–15** → Decision **December 16** → Start **January 1, 2027**.
