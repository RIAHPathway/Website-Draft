RIAH Pathway partners with high schools throughout all 50 states and Washington, D.C. to offer eligible students experiential pathways (Apprentice–Executive), entrepreneurship programs, certification review, applicable bar review, and educational pathways from minors through J.D., subject to each program's admission and eligibility requirements. The Standard Track uses the selected category's pricing; the two high-school dual-enrollment tracks remain General Education Only and First Bachelor's Degree. High school partners may also refer students who have left high school to RIAH Pathway's GED/HSE preparation programs, subject to GED/HSE eligibility. RIAH Pathway does not issue high school diplomas; partner high schools remain responsible for their diplomas.

## VIII. 1.8 — GED/HSE

### 1.8.1 — YOUR NEXT CHAPTER STARTS HERE.

**[IMAGE — GED/HSE SECTION VISUAL]**

**[ICON — GED/HSE SECTION ICON]**

Explore the current RIAH Pathway GED/HSE structure, options, and applicable eligibility, availability, and status disclosures.

GED/HSE preparation is available to applicants who have not completed high school, subject to eligibility and state requirements. People who left high school **before RIAH Pathway launched** are exempt from the two-completed-academic-year and 80%-attendance requirements. Those requirements apply to current high school students or applicants who withdrew within the preceding 12 months seeking the Second-Chance Pathway. That pathway excludes grade 8→9 transitions, ninth/tenth graders without two completed academic years, and students enrolled in the RIAH Diploma Program. Other applicants without a High School Partnership may seek the General GED/HSE Program. No 3.0 GPA minimum applies.

### 1.19.8 — QUESTION 6 — HIGH SCHOOL

#### DOES RIAH PATHWAY OFFER A HIGH SCHOOL PARTNERSHIP PATHWAY?

RIAH Pathway partners with high schools throughout all 50 states and Washington, D.C. to offer eligible students experiential pathways (Apprentice–Executive), entrepreneurship programs, certification review, applicable bar review, and educational pathways from minors through J.D., subject to each program's admission and eligibility requirements. The Standard Track uses the selected category's pricing; the two high-school dual-enrollment tracks remain General Education Only and First Bachelor's Degree. Other educational pathways, including minors, associate's, master's, MBA and J.D., use their respective applications. High school partners may also refer students who have left high school to RIAH Pathway's GED/HSE preparation programs, subject to GED/HSE eligibility. RIAH Pathway does not issue high school diplomas; partner high schools remain responsible for their diplomas.

Entry and transfer eligibility requirements apply.

Students may also explore eligible college dual-enrollment opportunities.

**[BUTTON — LEARN MORE → 7]**

### 1.19.9 — QUESTION 7 — GED/HSE

#### WHO IS ELIGIBLE FOR THE GED/HSE PATHWAY?

RIAH Pathway's GED/HSE structure provides a second-chance educational pathway for eligible applicants.

Applicants who left high school before RIAH Pathway launched are exempt from the two-year/80%-attendance rule. That rule applies to current high school students or recent withdrawals seeking the Second-Chance Pathway. GED/HSE Standard is a $100 four-core-course preparation option; the separate $1,500 option includes four embedded General Education college courses (History, Science, English, and Math) subject to applicable authorization and academic eligibility.
