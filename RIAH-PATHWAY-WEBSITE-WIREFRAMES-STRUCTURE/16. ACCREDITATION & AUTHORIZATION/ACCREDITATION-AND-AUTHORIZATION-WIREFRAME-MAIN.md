## BUILDING THE INSTITUTION BEFORE SEEKING THE RECOGNITION.

RIAH Pathway is building the academic, technological, governance, faculty, assessment, financial, compliance, student-service, authorization, and institutional-effectiveness infrastructure required to pursue applicable accreditation and recognition.

# IV. ACCREDITATION & AUTHORIZATION OVERVIEW VIDEO

## HOW RIAH PATHWAY IS BUILDING TOWARD ACCREDITATION

RIAH Pathway is building the institution before representing the institution as accredited.

The current phase includes institutional infrastructure, academic programs, curriculum architecture, faculty preparation, academic governance, student records, assessment controls, technology, financial documentation, authorization research, institutional effectiveness, and accreditation evidence.

The Founder is establishing the initial RIAH Pathway framework while the beta team, executive leadership, academic faculty, adjunct faculty, experiential professionals, technology personnel, and additional institutional personnel are recruited beginning in October 2026 and continuing as the ecosystem grows.

The Founder will also participate as a bona fide student in selected degree programs so RIAH Pathway can document the complete student lifecycle through actual institutional operations.

That lifecycle includes:

**APPLICATION → ADMISSION → ENROLLMENT → ORIENTATION → INSTRUCTION → ASSESSMENT → ACADEMIC PROGRESSION → COMPLETION → GRADUATION → OUTCOMES**

Founder status does not create an academic exception.

The Founder does not independently grade the Founder's own coursework, independently proctor the Founder's own assessments, award academic credit to the Founder, or independently approve the Founder's own program completion or graduation.

Qualified academic leaders and faculty will oversee curriculum, assessment, academic quality, faculty standards, student outcomes, and accreditation evidence before applicable accreditation reviews require those positions.

RIAH Pathway will pursue applicable recognition in stages.

Those stages include institutional accreditation, academic and programmatic accreditation, certificate-program accreditation, continuing-education accreditation, experiential-program recognition, state authorization, designations, and endorsements where applicable.

RIAH Pathway will clearly distinguish between a pathway being **planned**, **preparing**, **pursuing**, **applied**, **approved**, **authorized**, **designated**, **endorsed**, or **accredited**.
