# FOUNDATION SUPPORT CAN CONNECT TO APPROVED COMMUNITY ASSISTANCE.

The Products & Services wireframe includes a justice-impacted community pathway in which RIAH Pathway connects eligible participants to independent participating law firms. RIAH Pathway itself does not practice law or directly provide the independent legal services.

Where an approved Foundation program is active, qualifying students may be considered for need-based assistance with eligible attorney or court-related costs, subject to:

- Available Foundation funding
- Adopted eligibility requirements
- Independent Foundation review
- Applicable nonprofit requirements
- Partner-law-firm acceptance of the legal matter
- Applicable jurisdiction and court rules

Voluntary Foundation contributions must remain separate from attorney referral, representation, and legal-service access. A donation may not be required as consideration for an attorney introduction or representation.

No sealing, expungement, attorney acceptance, eligibility determination, or court outcome is guaranteed.
