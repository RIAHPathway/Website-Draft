|---|---:|

## 13 — PAYMENT OPTIONS

### PAY FOR YOUR PATHWAY IN THE WAY THAT APPLIES TO YOU.

[ICON CARDS — UPFRONT; MONTHLY; PER COURSE; SEMESTER]

**Upfront:** 15% eligible reduction subject to ordinary cap.

**Monthly:** up to six months per applicable paid course. The course must be completed and paid in full before the next paid course unlocks.

**Per Course:** applicable tuition divided by configured paid-course count; no invented course counts.

**Semester:** applicable when configured, allocating the established tuition.

## 14 — TITLE IV PAYMENT PATH

### SEMESTER-BASED FINANCIAL AID WHERE APPLICABLE.

[IMAGE — Semester financial-aid timeline]
