## ACCELERATED CURRICULUM VERIFICATION AND NON-WAIVABLE REQUIREMENTS

[ICON — COMPLIANCE AND COMPLETION VERIFICATION]

For eligible GED/HSE, High School, Associate's, Bachelor's, Minor, Master's, MBA, J.D. and approved state Non-J.D. academic coursework, a potential **one-month internal curriculum-completion** window requires documentation of all prescribed courses or approved transfers, prerequisites, applicable **80% minimum** objective assessments and performance assessments, assigned review courses meeting the **80% internal minimum**, proctoring, projects, documented supervision hours, capstones and applicable institutional checks. This promotes accessibility and affordability, **not an easier credential**. Mandatory state, accreditor, graduation and minimum study-duration rules override course pacing.

**Exceptions:** Professional Experiential and Entrepreneurship Experiential programs have **no acceleration** and must run for their published duration. Standalone certification and bar review coursework may advance at the learner's pace after all internal requirements are satisfied; external examinations and licenses remain separate.

## ASSESSMENT IS BUILT INTO THE CURRICULUM

RIAH Pathway maintains its own institutional assessments in addition to applicable assessments provided through instructional courseware.

### OBJECTIVE ASSESSMENTS

- 25 questions per applicable objective assessment.
- Proctored where required.
- Minimum passing score of 80%.

### PERFORMANCE ASSESSMENTS

- One task-based assessment where required.
- Proctored where required.
- Minimum passing score of 80%.

### ASSESSMENT ATTEMPTS

## CURRICULUM CULMINATES IN APPLICATION

### CAPSTONE COMPONENTS

- Objective Assessments.
- Performance Assessments.
- Cumulative Projects.
- Applied Work.
- Supervisor Meetings.
- Workbook Activities.
- Required Tasks.
- Testing and Deliverables.
- Professional Feedback.
- Revisions.
- Applied Builds.
- Capstone Presentations.
- Human Panel Review.

### SUPERVISION STANDARD

The controlling curriculum identifies twenty hours of supervision across applicable capstone courses, with two hours per course.

Industry-professional supervision applies to the designated Bachelor's Year 4, Master's, MBA, and Minor capstone courses.


The assigned supervisor is a qualified professional connected to the applicable field or major and provides the required meetings, direction, review, and feedback for the designated course. Faster academic progression does not waive the controlling supervision requirement or the existing hours allocation.
