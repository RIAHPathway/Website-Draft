## GENERAL EDUCATION PLACEMENT

Students may satisfy applicable General Education requirements through accepted transfer or alternative credit, or an applicable RIAH Pathway Placement or Test-Out Assessment.

### PLACEMENT STANDARD

| Requirement | Standard |
|---|---|
| Passing Score | 80% |
| Proctoring | Required |
| Maximum Attempts | 3 |
| Waiting Period | At least 24 hours between attempts |
| Successful Test-Out | Applicable requirement satisfied under the approved structure |
| Unsuccessful Attempt | No academic penalty |
| Three Unsuccessful Attempts | Complete the applicable RIAH course |
