### HOMELAND SECURITY CORE COURSE CONTENT

**HS 2101 — Foundations of Homeland Security**

Homeland Security foundations, institutions, responsibilities, and introductory security concepts.

Additional RIAH activities include Homeland Security research and applicable planning assignments.

**HS 2102 — Homeland Security Law, Policy and Ethics**

Legal frameworks, public policy, ethics, and Homeland Security responsibilities.

Additional activities include policy analysis and applicable legal or ethical research.

**HS 2103 — Principles of Governance, Risk and Compliance**

Governance, risk assessment, compliance controls, and organizational accountability.

Additional institutional curriculum includes compliance activities, software applications, and applicable compliance projects.

**HS 2104 — Principles of Intelligence**

Intelligence principles, research, analysis, and intelligence-related processes.

Additional institutional curriculum includes intelligence analysis and applicable analytical projects.

**HS 2105 — Principles of Physical Security**

Physical security fundamentals, facilities, protective measures, and security planning.

Additional institutional curriculum includes physical security applications and applicable planning projects.

**HS 2106 — Principles of Private Investigations**

Investigative methods, research, documentation, and investigative principles.

Additional institutional curriculum includes investigative activities and research assignments.

**HS 2107 — Emergency Management and Preparedness**

Emergency planning, preparedness, coordination, and response principles.

Additional institutional curriculum includes emergency planning and preparedness activities.

**HS 2108 — Critical Infrastructure Protection**

Critical infrastructure, risk, resilience, and protective planning.

Additional institutional curriculum includes infrastructure analysis and protective planning.

**HS 2109 — Security Operations and Incident Management**

Security operations, incident coordination, reporting, and incident management.

Additional institutional curriculum includes incident-management applications and projects.

**HS 2110 — Homeland Security Strategy and Coordination**

Homeland Security planning, organizational coordination, and strategy.

Additional institutional curriculum includes strategic planning and coordination projects.
