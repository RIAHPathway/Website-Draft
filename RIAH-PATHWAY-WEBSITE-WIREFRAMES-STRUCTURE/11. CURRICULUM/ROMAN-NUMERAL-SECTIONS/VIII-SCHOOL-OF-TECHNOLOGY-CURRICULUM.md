### TECHNOLOGY CORE COURSE CONTENT

**TEC 2101 — Principles of Computer Science**

Computing fundamentals, programming concepts, algorithms, and introductory computer science.

RIAH coursework includes programming activities and applicable projects.

**TEC 2102 — Web Development**

Web development fundamentals, website structure, styling, and interactive programming.

RIAH coursework includes web development activities and applicable website projects.

**TEC 2103 — Principles of Cybersecurity**

Cybersecurity fundamentals, security principles, risks, and introductory protective technologies.

RIAH coursework includes cybersecurity activities and applicable security projects.

**TEC 2104 — Principles of Information Systems**

Information systems, business technology, systems processes, and organizational information management.

RIAH coursework includes information systems activities and applicable systems projects.

**TEC 2105 — Operating Systems & Architecture**

Operating systems, computer architecture, system components, and introductory administration.

RIAH coursework includes operating system activities and applicable technical projects.

**TEC 2106 — Systems Analysis & Design**

Systems requirements, systems analysis, design methods, and technical documentation.

RIAH coursework includes systems analysis and applicable design projects.

**TEC 2107 — Principles of Data Analytics**

Data preparation, analysis, visualization, and introductory analytical methods.

RIAH coursework includes data analysis and applicable analytical projects.

**TEC 2108 — Principles of Data Science**

Data science fundamentals, data processing, computational analysis, and introductory methods.

RIAH coursework includes data science activities and applicable technical projects.

**TEC 2109 — Principles of Project Management**

Project planning, organization, scheduling, and management fundamentals.

RIAH coursework includes project planning and applicable management assignments.

**TEC 2110 — Principles of Program Management**

Program management principles, coordination of related projects, and program planning.

RIAH coursework includes program planning and applicable management projects.

**TEC 2111 — Principles of Software Development**

Software development fundamentals, programming, and introductory application development.

RIAH coursework includes development activities and applicable application projects.

**TEC 2112 — Principles of Software Engineering**

Software engineering principles, development processes, and software design.

RIAH coursework includes engineering activities and applicable design projects.

**TEC 2113 — Statistics**

Statistical methods, probability, interpretation, and quantitative analysis.

RIAH coursework includes statistical applications and applicable analytical assignments.
