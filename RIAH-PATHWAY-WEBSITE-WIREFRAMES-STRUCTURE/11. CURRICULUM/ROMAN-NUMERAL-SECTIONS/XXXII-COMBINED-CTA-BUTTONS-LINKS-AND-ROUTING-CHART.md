### ROUTING STANDARD

Curriculum content is displayed within the Curriculum website page.

Curriculum navigation labels, school curriculum cards, major curriculum headings, course tables, and curriculum architecture components are not buttons that redirect to the same Curriculum page.

Buttons route to the applicable related website section, admissions pathway, program page, Products page, Experiential page, student services, or authorized external system.
