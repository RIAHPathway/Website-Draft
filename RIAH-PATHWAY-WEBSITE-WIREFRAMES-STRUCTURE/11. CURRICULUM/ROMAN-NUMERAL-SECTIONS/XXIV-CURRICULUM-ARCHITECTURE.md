## THE STRUCTURE BEHIND THE CURRICULUM

### COURSE NUMBERING

Established course codes remain attached to the applicable curriculum and academic level.

### PREREQUISITES

Students progress through the established prerequisite chain.

### CERTIFICATION MAPPING

Applicable courses identify the corresponding certification track, learning progression, and review requirements.

### ASSESSMENT

The approved course record determines which institutional objective assessments, performance assessments, midterms, finals, and additional academic activities apply.

### PROJECTS

Applicable curriculum contains academic and professional projects.

### CAPSTONES

Applicable designated courses contain cumulative assessments, projects, supervision, and review.

### EXPERIENTIAL INTEGRATION

Applicable coursework connects to professional activities, placement eligibility, supervision, and performance documentation.

### ENTREPRENEURSHIP CURRICULUM INTEGRATION

[ICON — ENTREPRENEURSHIP / APPLIED BUSINESS MILESTONES]

The separate Entrepreneurship curriculum combines institutional business learning with actual startup or small-business tasks, implementation milestones, qualified professional collaboration, level-specific assessment, applied business capstones, and a documented completion review.
