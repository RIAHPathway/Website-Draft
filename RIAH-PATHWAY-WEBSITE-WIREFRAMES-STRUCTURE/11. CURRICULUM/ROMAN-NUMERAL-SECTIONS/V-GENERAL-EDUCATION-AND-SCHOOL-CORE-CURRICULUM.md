### COURSE CONTENT

**ENG 1010 — College English**

Academic writing, reading comprehension, grammar, composition, research, and written communication.

**MAT 1010 — College Algebra**

Algebraic expressions, equations, functions, graphs, and quantitative reasoning.

**COM 1010 — Oral Communications**

Public speaking, presentations, interpersonal communication, and communication techniques.

**LAN 1010 — Foreign Language**

Language fundamentals, vocabulary, grammar, reading, writing, and communication.

**HIS 1010 — History**

Historical developments, institutions, events, and historical analysis.

**PHI 1010 — Philosophy**

Philosophical reasoning, ethics, logic, argument analysis, and major philosophical questions.


**PHI 1010 instructional mapping:** Philosophy remains a required General Education course. The uploaded curriculum wireframe identifies a gap in the mapped Edmentum Philosophy courseware; RIAH additional curriculum and other approved resources supply the applicable instructional coverage without removing PHI 1010 or changing its course identity.

**PSY 1010 — Psychology**

Human behavior, cognition, learning, development, and psychological concepts.

**SOC 1010 — Sociology**

Social institutions, communities, social behavior, culture, and social structures.

**ART 1010 — Art**

Artistic expression, interpretation, historical context, and visual analysis.

**SCI 1010 — Science**

Scientific principles, scientific reasoning, observation, and analysis.

### GENERAL EDUCATION LEARNING MATERIALS

RIAH Pathway uses the applicable provider's instructional curriculum while maintaining its own additional curriculum.

For these courses, RIAH's additional instruction may include writing assignments, research, problem-solving activities, communication activities, laboratory-related activities, case analysis, and applicable course projects.

The institutional assessment standards are specified in Section XI.
