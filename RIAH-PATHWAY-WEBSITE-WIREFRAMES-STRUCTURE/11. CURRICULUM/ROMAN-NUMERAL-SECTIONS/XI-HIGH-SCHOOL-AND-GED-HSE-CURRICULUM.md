## HIGH SCHOOL DIPLOMA

**Named School:** School of Diploma. The existing approved High School Diploma curriculum requirements remain unchanged.

### HIGH SCHOOL CURRICULUM

The RIAH Pathway High School Diploma curriculum includes:

- Four academic years.
- 40 fixed courses.
- 120 RIAH credit hours.
- Applicable state-specific curriculum components.
- Regular High School and Dual Enrollment pathways.
- Applicable Edmentum instructional content.
- Additional RIAH Pathway curriculum.
- Required academic assessments.
- Applicable projects.
- Applicable midterm and final examinations.


### SECONDARY COURSEWARE AND ASSESSMENT MAPPING

[ICON — DIPLOMA / SECONDARY EDUCATION]

Edmentum courseware is mapped into applicable High School Diploma courses as an instructional resource, while the RIAH course curriculum controls additional assignments, state components, assessments, and progression. Proctored objective assessments, performance assessments, or both apply according to the approved high-school course record; the curriculum's controlling assessment requirements are not replaced by the instructional provider.

### HIGH SCHOOL LEARNING STRUCTURE

[ICON — HIGH SCHOOL ASSESSMENT]

High School coursework follows the applicable minimum 80% passing standard for required assessments. Approved course completion, assessment outcomes, any state-specific requirements, and graduation eligibility control progression.

### HIGH SCHOOL ADMISSIONS

Applicants entering RIAH High School from eighth grade must meet the established ninth-grade entry requirements, including an eighth-grade GPA of at least 3.0.

Students entering at eleventh grade or later may be considered for transfer after completing two high school academic years and meeting the applicable cumulative GPA requirement.

### DUAL ENROLLMENT

Dual Enrollment combines applicable High School coursework with college-level General Education and School Core requirements.

High School and college credits remain separately recorded.

Applicants under eighteen must meet applicable parent or guardian and guidance counselor approval requirements.

## GED/HSE PREPARATION

**Named School:** School of GED. The existing GED/HSE preparation and concurrent-college-credit requirements remain unchanged.

[ICON — GED PREPARATION]

[IMAGE — Learner completing GED/HSE practice materials.]

RIAH Pathway's GED/HSE curriculum supports preparation in applicable subject areas.

### GED/HSE LEARNING STRUCTURE

[ICON — GED/HSE ACADEMIC PREPARATION]

Academic preparation spans Language Arts, Mathematics, Science, and Social Studies with applicable practice, institutional assessments, readiness monitoring, and instructional progress review. Preparation does not confer an official GED or HiSET credential; the applicable official exam and issuing authority govern credential issuance.

- Applicable Edmentum instructional curriculum.
- RIAH Pathway additional preparation curriculum.
- Practice activities.
- Internal assessments.
- Applicable learning support.
- Preparation for the official examination.

### GED/HSE ELIGIBILITY

The established RIAH GED/HSE pathway is designed for eligible individuals who left high school before the institutional launch and have not received a diploma or GED.

Eligibility includes:

- Two completed high school academic years.
- At least 80% attendance in each completed year.
- No existing high school diploma or GED.
- Applicable pre-launch eligibility requirements.

There is no 3.0 GPA requirement for the GED/HSE pathway.

### CONCURRENT COLLEGE CREDIT

Eligible GED/HSE students may pursue up to twelve concurrent college General Education credits.

GED/HSE preparation and college credits remain separate academic records.
