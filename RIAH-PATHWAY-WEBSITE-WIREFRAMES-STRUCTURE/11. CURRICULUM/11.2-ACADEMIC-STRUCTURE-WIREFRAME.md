## EDUCATION PATHWAYS: TYPICAL DURATIONS AND ACCELERATION

[ICON — ACADEMIC ACCESS AND ACCELERATED COMPLETION]

RIAH Pathway supports academically accelerated progress in eligible GED/HSE preparation, High School, Associate's, Bachelor's, Minor, Master's, MBA, J.D., and state-specific Non-J.D. coursework. The objective is **accessible, affordable education without making courses easier**. An eligible student may complete internal curriculum in as little as **one month**, but only if all mandatory coursework and required conditions can actually be completed in that period and no applicable legal, accreditation, school or other minimum time requirement prevents the award.

Typical program durations: GED/HSE **preparation approximately one year**; High School **four years**; Associate's **two years**; Bachelor's **four years**; Minor **one semester to one year**; Master's **one year**; MBA **one year**; J.D. **four years**. State Non-J.D. law-study paths vary: California Law Office Study requires **four years** of supervised study, while Maine requires at least **one year in a Maine attorney's office after completing two-thirds of an ABA-accredited law school's program**. Maine's one-year component does not represent the whole law-study pathway. The applicable state and accreditor minimum duration is controlling; a one-month internal coursework target never guarantees a one-month J.D. or state bar eligibility.

Students must complete every course or obtain approved transfer credit, meet prerequisites, achieve **80% minimum** on applicable objective assessments (OA) and performance assessments (PA) and on assigned internal review courses, and fulfill exams, applied projects, documented supervision hours, and required capstones. **Professional Experiential and Entrepreneurship Experiential cannot be accelerated.** Standalone certification and bar-review courses can advance at the student's pace while retaining their required internal 80% standard.

[FLOW — REQUIRED OR APPROVED TRANSFERRED COURSES → PREREQUISITES → OA + PA AT 80% MINIMUM → REQUIRED REVIEW COURSES AT 80% MINIMUM → APPLICABLE SUPERVISION HOURS + CAPSTONES → COMPLIANCE REVIEW → APPROVED COMPLETION]

### ACCELERATION

One month is the earliest potential curriculum-completion timeframe where permitted by the applicable program requirements.

It is not a guaranteed degree or credential completion period.

Acceleration does not remove:

- Prerequisites.
- Required coursework.
- Objective assessments.
- Performance assessments.
- Applicable projects.
- Applicable midterms.
- Applicable finals.
- Required proctoring.
- Capstones.
- Professional supervision.
- Payment requirements.
- Mandatory duration requirements.
- External credentialing requirements.


Acceleration changes academic pacing, not curriculum obligations. Applicable simulations, assigned curriculum products and learning-resource controls, experiential experience requirements, and approved course-level supervision remain in force when a student progresses faster.

## CURRICULUM CULMINATES IN APPLICATION

### CAPSTONE COMPONENTS

- Objective Assessments.
- Performance Assessments.

- Applied Work.

- Workbook Activities.
- Required Tasks.
- Testing and Deliverables.
- Professional Feedback.
- Revisions.
- Applied Builds.
- Capstone Presentations.
- Human Panel Review.

### SUPERVISION STANDARD

The controlling curriculum identifies twenty hours of supervision across applicable capstone courses, with two hours per course.

Industry-professional supervision applies to the designated Bachelor's Year 4, Master's, MBA, and Minor capstone courses.


The assigned supervisor is a qualified professional connected to the applicable field or major and provides the required meetings, direction, review, and feedback for the designated course. Faster academic progression does not waive the controlling supervision requirement or the existing hours allocation.
