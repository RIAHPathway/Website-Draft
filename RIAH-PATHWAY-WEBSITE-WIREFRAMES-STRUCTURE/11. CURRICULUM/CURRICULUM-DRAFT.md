## CURRICULUM

# STRUCTURED ACADEMICS.
# APPLIED LEARNING.
# PROFESSIONAL PROGRESSION.

RIAH Pathway uses structured academic pathways with established General Education, School Core, major, minor, graduate, law, secondary-school, and GED/HSE preparation structures.

Academic pathways follow established curriculum sequences rather than unrestricted student-selected coursework.

RIAH Pathway combines academic education, professional software, certification review, projects, experiential learning, applied builds, supervision, capstones, career preparation, and law-related preparation where applicable.

Students progress through the curriculum associated with their selected school, major, degree, or educational pathway.

### CURRICULUM OWNERSHIP

RIAH Pathway develops and maintains its own institutional curriculum while incorporating applicable instructional materials from educational providers such as:

- Edmentum.
- Cengage MindTap.
- McGraw Hill.
- CALI.
- Other approved instructional providers.

Students complete applicable provider curriculum alongside RIAH Pathway's additional academic curriculum.

Provider instructional materials supplement the institutional curriculum without replacing RIAH Pathway's academic requirements.

RIAH Pathway maintains its own:

- Course materials.
- Academic activities.
- Objective assessments.
- Performance assessments.
- Professional software assignments.
- Projects.
- Certification preparation.
- Capstones.
- Applied builds.
- Experiential learning.
- Supervision requirements.
- Applicable midterm examinations.
- Applicable final examinations.

The specific requirements depend on the approved course curriculum.

Not every course includes every assessment, project, midterm, final examination, or capstone.

### MINOR CURRICULUM

**Standard Minor Size:** 15 Credits.

**Typical Structure:** Five courses worth three credits each.

**Course Selection:** Predetermined.

**Unrestricted Student-Selected Electives:** Not part of the established minor architecture.

**Transfer Maximum:** Six credits.

Minor admission requires completion of the first required minor course and its applicable prerequisites.

For example, a Computer Science minor applicant must satisfy College Algebra and Principles of Computer Science before the applicable minor admission gateway.

### BACHELOR'S CURRICULUM

**General Education:** 30 credits.

**School Core:** Applicable school-specific foundation.

**Year 3 Major:** 30 credits.

**Year 4 Major:** 30 credits.

Students must satisfy applicable General Education and School Core requirements before Year 3 admission.

Year 3 and Year 4 major coursework remains RIAH-controlled.

### MASTER'S AND MBA

Master's and MBA applicants must satisfy the applicable prerequisites and hold a corresponding bachelor's degree or accepted equivalent qualification.

Graduate curriculum includes the assigned professional software, academic assessments, projects, and applicable capstone.
