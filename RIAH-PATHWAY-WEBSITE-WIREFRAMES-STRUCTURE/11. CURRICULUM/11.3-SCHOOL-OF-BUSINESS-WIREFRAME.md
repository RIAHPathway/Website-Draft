### BUSINESS CORE COURSE CONTENT

**BUS 2101 — Principles of Financial Accounting**

Financial accounting principles, transactions, financial statements, and introductory accounting analysis.

RIAH coursework includes additional financial statement analysis and applicable accounting activities.

**BUS 2102 — Principles of Managerial Accounting**

Internal accounting information, managerial decisions, costs, budgets, and financial performance.

RIAH coursework includes budgeting exercises and applicable managerial accounting activities.

**BUS 2103 — Microeconomics**

Supply, demand, market behavior, pricing, and individual economic decisions.

RIAH coursework includes additional economic analysis and applicable research assignments.

**BUS 2104 — Macroeconomics**

Economic growth, inflation, employment, monetary systems, and economic policy.

RIAH coursework includes additional macroeconomic analysis and applicable research projects.

**BUS 2105 — Statistics**

Statistical methods, data interpretation, probability, and quantitative business analysis.

RIAH coursework includes additional statistical applications and applicable analytical activities.

**BUS 2106 — Marketing**

Marketing principles, customer research, marketing strategy, and promotional planning.

RIAH coursework includes customer research, marketing activities, and applicable marketing projects.

**BUS 2107 — Principles of Management**

Organizational management, leadership, planning, and business operations.

RIAH coursework includes management activities and applicable organizational projects.

**BUS 2108 — Operations Management**

Operational planning, processes, resources, productivity, and performance improvement.

RIAH coursework includes workflow analysis and applicable process improvement activities.

**BUS 2109 — Principles of Entrepreneurship**

Business creation, entrepreneurship, business models, and early-stage business planning.

RIAH coursework includes entrepreneurship activities and applicable business planning projects.

**BUS 2110 — Principles of Finance**

Financial management, financial decisions, financial analysis, and introductory investment concepts.

RIAH coursework includes additional financial analysis and applicable financial projects.
