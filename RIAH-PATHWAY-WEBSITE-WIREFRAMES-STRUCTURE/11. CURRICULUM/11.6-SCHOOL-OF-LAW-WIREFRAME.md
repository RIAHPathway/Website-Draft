### LAW CORE COURSE CONTENT

**LAW 2001 — Introduction to Law & Legal Systems**

Legal systems, sources of law, legal institutions, and introductory legal concepts.

RIAH coursework includes additional legal analysis and applicable research assignments.

**LAW 2002 — Principles of Criminal Justice**

Criminal justice systems, institutions, responsibilities, and introductory principles.

RIAH coursework includes criminal justice analysis and applicable projects.

**LAW 2003 — Criminal Law**

Criminal law principles, criminal offenses, legal elements, and criminal responsibility.

RIAH coursework includes criminal law analysis and applicable legal research assignments.

**LAW 2004 — Courts & Judicial Systems**

Court structures, judicial responsibilities, court procedures, and the judicial system.

RIAH coursework includes court system analysis and applicable research projects.

**LAW 2005 — Policing & Law Enforcement**

Law enforcement institutions, policing principles, responsibilities, and procedures.

RIAH coursework includes law enforcement analysis and applicable research assignments.

**LAW 2006 — Corrections & Rehabilitation**

Corrections systems, rehabilitation, institutional responsibilities, and correctional policy.

RIAH coursework includes corrections analysis and applicable research projects.

**LAW 2007 — Criminal Investigation & Evidence**

Criminal investigations, evidence principles, investigative procedures, and documentation.

RIAH coursework includes investigation analysis and applicable evidence-related projects.

**LAW 2008 — Criminology**

Crime, criminal behavior, criminological theories, and analysis.

RIAH coursework includes criminological analysis and applicable research assignments.

**LAW 2009 — Ethics in Criminal Justice**

Professional ethics, responsibilities, ethical decisions, and criminal justice accountability.

RIAH coursework includes ethics analysis and applicable case studies.

**LAW 2010 — Criminal Justice Research & Analysis**

Criminal justice research, analytical methods, evidence evaluation, and reporting.

RIAH coursework includes additional research activities and applicable analytical projects.

## J.D. AND NON-J.D. CURRICULUM INTEGRATION

The J.D. and Non-J.D. pathways remain separate programs while utilizing the applicable RIAH legal curriculum, products, and review architecture.

The academic and jurisdictional requirements surrounding shared legal content differ by pathway.

Applicable supervision, legal-study documentation, examination requirements, and state rules remain controlling.


RIAH does not substitute a general J.D. supervision policy for the jurisdiction-specific study and supervision requirements that govern a Non-J.D. law pathway. External licensing eligibility and examinations remain independently governed by the applicable jurisdiction.
