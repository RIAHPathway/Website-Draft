## IV. STARTUP ENTREPRENEURSHIP EXPERIENTIAL — 6.2

Startup applicants must demonstrate a **developed business model**, relevant market research, projected budget, and defined product/service, prototype or MVP. An undeveloped idea alone does not qualify.

### 6.2.1 — Apprentice Startup

**Eligibility:** Pre-launch or just starting. **Duration:** **1 Month**. **Focus:** Business model, launch budget, market research, operating readiness.

| Category | Applied work / milestone |
| --- | --- |
| Business model | Refine documented proposed product/service and business model |
| Market | Identify target customers, competition and market evidence |
| Finance | Prepare initial budget and projections |
| Operations | Review formation and basic business systems |
| Final | Launch-readiness plan and assessment |

**Completion:** Documented coursework, applied business deliverables, supervisor/reviewer assessment and capstone.

### 6.2.2 — New Startup

**Eligibility:** Operating less than 1 year. **Duration:** **12 Weeks**. **Focus:** Financial records, operations, marketing and sales.

| Category | Applied work / milestone |
| --- | --- |
| Operations | Set up workflows and relevant records |
| Finance | Bookkeeping and cash-flow monitoring |
| Sales | Introduce early marketing and sales processes |
| Technology | Implement appropriate systems |
| Final | Documented early-stage operations plan |

**Completion:** Documented coursework, applied business deliverables, supervisor/reviewer assessment and capstone.

### 6.2.3 — One-Year Startup

**Eligibility:** Operating at least 1 year. **Duration:** **12 Weeks**. **Focus:** Operational assessment, financial review and improvement.

| Category | Applied work / milestone |
| --- | --- |
| Diagnostic | Assess first-year operations |
| Finance | Evaluate revenue, expenses and cash flow |
| Customer | Review retention and acquisition |
| Improvement | Implement priority operational changes |
| Final | Measured stabilization and improvement plan |

**Completion:** Documented coursework, applied business deliverables, supervisor/reviewer assessment and capstone.

### 6.2.4 — Growth Startup

**Eligibility:** Operating more than 1 year; growth-ready. **Duration:** **16 Weeks**. **Focus:** Scale, management, technology and expansion.

| Category | Applied work / milestone |
| --- | --- |
| Strategy | Plan additional markets, customers or services |
| Finance | Develop forecasts and revenue-growth metrics |
| Management | Assess staff and scalable workflows |
| Technology | Evaluate automation, cybersecurity and infrastructure |
| Final | Implemented growth strategy and performance review |

**Completion:** Documented coursework, applied business deliverables, supervisor/reviewer assessment and capstone.



## V. SMALL BUSINESS ENTREPRENEURSHIP EXPERIENTIAL — 6.3

Small-business applicants must provide applicable registration/ownership details, evidence of operations where established, financial or operating information and decision-maker commitment to actual applied work.

### 6.3.1 — Apprentice Small Business

**Eligibility:** Forming or formally restarting. **Duration:** **1 Month**. **Focus:** Operating foundations and business planning.

| Category | Applied work / milestone |
| --- | --- |
| Readiness | Establish business operating plan |
| Finance | Prepare budgets and accounting foundations |
| Registration | Review applicable business requirements |
| Customers | Identify initial customers and marketing |
| Final | Documented operating readiness |

**Completion:** Documented coursework, applied business deliverables, supervisor/reviewer assessment and capstone.

### 6.3.2 — New Small Business

**Eligibility:** Operating less than 1 year. **Duration:** **12 Weeks**. **Focus:** Customer development, recordkeeping and stabilization.

| Category | Applied work / milestone |
| --- | --- |
| Operations | Improve early operations and service delivery |
| Accounting | Set up bookkeeping and cash-flow practices |
| Marketing | Build initial customer acquisition |
| Management | Set responsibilities and operating policies |
| Final | Stabilization action plan |

**Completion:** Documented coursework, applied business deliverables, supervisor/reviewer assessment and capstone.

### 6.3.3 — Established Small Business

**Eligibility:** Operating at least 1 year. **Duration:** **12 Weeks**. **Focus:** Efficiency, management and operational improvements.

| Category | Applied work / milestone |
| --- | --- |
| Assessment | Review ongoing business performance |
| Finance | Analyze expenses, revenue and cash flow |
| Systems | Assess procedures and technology needs |
| Customers | Improve retention and acquisition |
| Final | Measured process improvements |

**Completion:** Documented coursework, applied business deliverables, supervisor/reviewer assessment and capstone.

### 6.3.4 — Small Business Recovery & Growth

**Eligibility:** Operating more than 1 year; recovery/growth needs. **Duration:** **16 Weeks**. **Focus:** Business recovery, restructuring and sustainable growth.

| Category | Applied work / milestone |
| --- | --- |
| Diagnostic | Assess recovery and growth needs |
| Cash flow | Plan revenue and expense improvements |
| Restructuring | Implement operational improvements |
| Partners | Coordinate qualified specialists where approved |
| Final | Recovery/growth capstone and long-term plan |

**Completion:** Documented coursework, applied business deliverables, supervisor/reviewer assessment and capstone.

## XII. UPFRONT / MONTHLY PAYMENT AND CANCELLATION — 6.10

| Published offering | Standard tuition | Pay upfront: 25% off | Balance after $500 deposit credit | Monthly full tuition |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Startup Launch — 12 weeks | $5,000 | $3,750 | $3,250 | 3 installments: $1,666.67 / $1,666.67 / $1,666.66 |
| Small Business Recovery & Growth — 16 weeks | $10,000 | $7,500 | $7,000 | 4 installments of $2,500 |

The $500 deposit reduces the tuition balance under either payment option and is **not an extra tuition charge**. The Entrepreneurship 25% upfront reduction is separate from academic/Professional Experiential tuition reductions.

**Monthly nonpayment:** Pause program participation, course/curriculum access, program-managed professional collaboration and applied business work until the account is current.

**Upfront cancellation:** Cancellation is permitted; amounts for delivered/used services constitute earned revenue, while unused prepaid tuition is refunded subject to the applicable published agreement and law. Existing four-week discounted program-period values are $1,250 (Startup Launch) and $1,875 (Small Business Recovery & Growth), plus applicable partial periods under the published terms.
