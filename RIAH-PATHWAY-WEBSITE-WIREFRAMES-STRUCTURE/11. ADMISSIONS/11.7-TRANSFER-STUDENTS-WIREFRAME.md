# III. TRANSFER CREDIT MAXIMUMS AND REQUIREMENTS

| Program | Transfer Credits | Requirements and Restrictions |
|---|---|---|
| Associate's | Up to 30 credits | Approved General Education, School Core, or applicable foundational coursework. |
| Bachelor's Years 1–2 | Up to 60 credits | Approved General Education and School Core courses. School-specific requirements control. |
| Bachelor's Year 3 | 0 outside credits | Complete required General Education and School Core before admission. Year 3 major coursework is RIAH-controlled. |
| Bachelor's Year 4 | 0 outside credits | Complete Year 3 first. Year 4 cumulative curriculum, capstone, project, and supervision are RIAH-controlled. |
| Minor | 3 credits per approved foundational course; up to 6 credits for two approved courses | Eligible foundational courses applicable to the selected minor. |
| Master's | Up to 9 credits | Approved graduate coursework subject to program equivalency. |
| MBA | Up to 9 credits | Approved graduate coursework subject to program equivalency. |
| J.D. | Up to 27 credits | Eligible first-year 1L coursework from an approved qualifying law school. |
| J.D. Years 2–4 | 0 outside credits | RIAH-controlled coursework and program requirements. |
| Non-J.D. | Case-by-case | Eligible previous law-study coursework and documented supervision hours, subject to state requirements. |
| High School Grades 9–12 | Based on completed credits | Transfer credits evaluated according to the student's grade level and completed coursework. |
| High School Entering After Grade 8 | Based on applicable prior coursework | Complete RIAH Grades 9–12 curriculum through Regular or Dual Enrollment. |
| GED/HSE | No transfer into the four required preparation courses | Four preparation courses, applicable state-specific requirements, and optional concurrent General Education credit. |
| Experiential | Program-specific | Prior learning and experience reviewed for eligibility and placement under the selected experiential program. |
