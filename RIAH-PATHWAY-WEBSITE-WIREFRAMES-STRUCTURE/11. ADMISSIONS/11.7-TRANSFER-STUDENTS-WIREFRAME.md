# II. TRANSFER ADMISSIONS OVERVIEW

## YOUR EDUCATION. YOUR CREDITS. YOUR PATHWAY.

RIAH Pathway evaluates approved prior coursework, alternative credits, examinations, and applicable professional learning to determine each student's academic placement.

| Component | Requirement |
|---|---|
| Eligible Applicants | High school, GED/HSE, associate's, bachelor's, minor, master's, MBA, J.D., and Non-J.D. applicants. |
| Transfer Evaluation | Individual evaluation of submitted academic records. |
| Credit Approval | Approved course equivalency and applicable program requirements. |
| Alternative Credits | Accepted sources identified within the General Curriculum Structure. |
| Entry Point | Based on approved credits and completed prerequisites. |
| Missing Courses | Complete outstanding requirements through RIAH. |
| Acceleration | Students may progress faster after completing applicable requirements. |
| Tuition | Same published program tuition with or without transfer credits. |
| Transfer Tuition Reduction | **$0** |
| Student Allocation Deposit | Program-specific amount covering applicable enrollment resources and student services. |
| Transfer Evaluation Cost | Included in the student allocation deposit when applicable. |
| Application Fee | Separate applicable application fee. |
| Products | Separate published product pricing. |
| Academic Standards | Same required coursework, assessments, projects, and supervision. |
| Monthly Cohorts | Eligible students enter the applicable monthly cohort. |
| Personalized Curriculum | Remaining coursework and materials determined through the academic evaluation. |

# III. TRANSFER CREDIT MAXIMUMS AND REQUIREMENTS

| Program | Transfer Credits | Requirements and Restrictions |
|---|---|---|
| Associate's | Up to 30 credits | Approved General Education, School Core, or applicable foundational coursework. |
| Bachelor's Years 1–2 | Up to 60 credits | Approved General Education and School Core courses. School-specific requirements control. |
| Bachelor's Year 3 | 0 outside credits | Complete required General Education and School Core before admission. Year 3 major coursework is RIAH-controlled. |
| Bachelor's Year 4 | 0 outside credits | Complete Year 3 first. Year 4 cumulative curriculum, capstone, project, and supervision are RIAH-controlled. |
| Minor | 3 credits per approved foundational course; up to 6 credits for two approved courses | Eligible foundational courses applicable to the selected minor. |
| Master's | Up to 9 credits | Approved graduate coursework subject to program equivalency. |
| MBA | Up to 9 credits | Approved graduate coursework subject to program equivalency. |
| J.D. | Up to 27 credits | Eligible first-year 1L coursework from an approved qualifying law school. |
| J.D. Years 2–4 | 0 outside credits | RIAH-controlled coursework and program requirements. |
| Non-J.D. | Case-by-case | Eligible previous law-study coursework and documented supervision hours, subject to state requirements. |
| High School Grades 9–12 | Based on completed credits | Transfer credits evaluated according to the student's grade level and completed coursework. |
| High School Entering After Grade 8 | Based on applicable prior coursework | Complete RIAH Grades 9–12 curriculum through Regular or Dual Enrollment. |
| GED/HSE | No transfer into the four required preparation courses | Four preparation courses, applicable state-specific requirements, and optional concurrent General Education credit. |
| Experiential | Program-specific | Prior learning and experience reviewed for eligibility and placement under the selected experiential program. |

### Minor Transfer Credit Examples

| Minor | Approved Foundational Course | Credits | Transfer Evaluation |
|---|---|---|---|
| Computer Science | Principles of Computer Science | 3 | Approved alternative or prior college credit. |
| Accounting | Principles of Financial Accounting | 3 | Approved alternative or prior college credit. |
| Accounting | Principles of Managerial Accounting | 3 | Approved alternative or prior college credit. |
| Accounting | Financial Accounting + Managerial Accounting | 6 | Both courses require approval and prerequisite review. |
| Computer Science | Two eligible foundational Computer Science courses | 6 | Both courses must satisfy the minor curriculum. |
| Other Approved Minors | Applicable foundational coursework | 3–6 | Based on approved required courses. |

### Minor Transfer Restrictions

| Requirement | Policy |
|---|---|
| Standard Minor Curriculum | 15 credits unless the applicable approved curriculum specifies otherwise. |
| Foundational Course | 3 credits per approved course. |
| Two Foundational Courses | Up to 6 approved credits. |
| Alternative Credit Sources | Accepted according to RIAH's school-specific equivalency tables. |
| Advanced Minor Coursework | Complete through RIAH. |
| Course 300A | RIAH Applied Learning. |
| Course 300B | RIAH Applied Learning Capstone. |
| Capstone Transfer | Not accepted. |
| Professional Supervision | Required for designated applied-learning and capstone work. |
| Credit Duplication | The same completed course cannot receive duplicate credit. |

### Minor Enrollment Options

| Enrollment Type | Requirements | Pricing |
|---|---|---|
| Minor Within a Bachelor's Program | An enrolled bachelor's student selects an eligible minor as part of the academic pathway. | Follow the applicable bachelor's tuition and enrollment structure. |
| Standalone Minor | A student enrolls specifically to complete an approved minor without completing a RIAH bachelor's degree. | Standalone minor tuition and allocation deposit to be finalized. |
| Minor Transfer Student | Eligible foundational courses evaluated before placement. | Follow the applicable enrolled program's pricing structure. |
| Minor Academic Requirements | Complete all outstanding minor courses, including designated 300A and 300B requirements. | Required academic resources included within the applicable allocation structure. |
| Minor Products | Applicable textbooks, workbooks, journals, and other academic materials. | Included allocation materials or separately priced products, as applicable. |

### Minor Pricing Follow-Up

| Item | Required Update |
|---|---|
| Minor Within Bachelor's | Confirm the applicable bachelor's enrollment and allocation structure. |
| Standalone Minor | Finalize standalone minor tuition. |
| Standalone Minor Allocation | Finalize the required student allocation deposit. |
| Application Fee | Confirm the applicable application fee. |
| Transfer Evaluation | Include within the allocation deposit when applicable. |
| Products | Apply the separately published product pricing where applicable. |

# IV. BACHELOR'S YEAR 3 PREPARATION — HIGHLY RECOMMENDED

## COMPLETE YOUR FOUNDATION BEFORE YEAR 3.

| Requirement | Description |
|---|---|
| Recommendation | **Highly recommended:** Complete General Education and School Core before applying for Year 3. |
| General Education | 30 credits. |
| School Core | 30 credits under the standard structure. |
| Standard Foundational Coursework | **60 credits: 30 General Education + 30 School Core.** |
| School-Specific Requirements | Complete the actual core established by the school. |
| School of Technology Core | 39 credits. |
| Technology Foundation | 30 General Education credits plus the applicable 39-credit Technology Core. |
| Credit Sources | Approved alternative credits, examination scores, prior college coursework, and RIAH placement. |
| Missing Credits | Complete outstanding foundational requirements through RIAH. |
| Year 3 Admission | All applicable General Education and School Core prerequisites must be completed. |
| Year 3 Curriculum | Required major coursework. |
| Year 4 Admission | Successful completion of Year 3 and applicable prerequisites. |
| Year 4 Curriculum | Upper-division multidisciplinary curriculum, cumulative project, proctored capstone assessments, and professional supervision. |
| Applicable Majors | Year 4 cumulative project and capstone requirements apply across bachelor's majors. |

### Bachelor's Academic Structure

| Academic Year | Credits | Curriculum | Transfer Eligibility |
|---|---|---|---|
| Year 1 | 30 | General Education | Approved transfer or alternative credits. |
| Year 2 | 30 standard; school-specific core may differ | School Core | Approved transfer or alternative credits. |
| Year 3 | 30 standard major credits | Major Curriculum | RIAH coursework only. |
| Year 4 | 30 standard upper-division credits | Integrated Curriculum + Cumulative Project + Capstone | RIAH coursework only. |
| Standard Bachelor's Structure | 120 credits | Four-year curriculum | Subject to the selected school's requirements. |
