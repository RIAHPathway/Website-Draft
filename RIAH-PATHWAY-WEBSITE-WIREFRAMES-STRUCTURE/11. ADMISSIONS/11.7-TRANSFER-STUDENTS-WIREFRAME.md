# III. TRANSFER CREDIT MAXIMUMS AND REQUIREMENTS

| Program | Transfer Credits | Requirements and Restrictions |
|---|---|---|
| Associate's | Up to 30 credits | Approved General Education, School Core, or applicable foundational coursework. |
| Bachelor's Years 1–2 | Up to 60 credits | Approved General Education and School Core courses. School-specific requirements control. |
| Bachelor's Year 3 | 0 outside credits | Complete required General Education and School Core before admission. Year 3 major coursework is RIAH-controlled. |
| Bachelor's Year 4 | 0 outside credits | Complete Year 3 first. Year 4 cumulative curriculum, capstone, project, and supervision are RIAH-controlled. |
| Minor | 3 credits per approved foundational course; up to 6 credits for two approved courses | Eligible foundational courses applicable to the selected minor. |
| Master's | Up to 9 credits | Approved graduate coursework subject to program equivalency. Applicants without a bachelor's degree **in the selected major** or equivalent subject preparation (for example, a Master's in Computer Science applicant without a Computer Science bachelor's) complete identified **major-specific foundational prerequisites** through RIAH or RIAH-approved alternative/transfer course equivalencies before the applicable graduate curriculum. Prerequisite satisfaction is evaluated separately from the **up to 9 graduate transfer credits**; all other admissions requirements still apply. |
| MBA | Up to 9 credits | Approved graduate coursework subject to program equivalency. Applicants without corresponding field preparation complete applicable prerequisites through RIAH or approved alternative/transfer equivalencies. **Computer Science MBA example:** a management-focused Computer Science MBA still requires applicable **foundational technical Computer Science coursework** to understand and manage Computer Science systems, work, and teams; it is not a waiver of technical preparation. Prerequisite satisfaction is separate from the **up to 9 graduate transfer credits**; all other admissions requirements still apply. |
| J.D. | Up to 27 credits | Eligible first-year 1L coursework from an approved qualifying law school. |
| J.D. Years 2–4 | 0 outside credits | RIAH-controlled coursework and program requirements. |
| Non-J.D. | Case-by-case | Eligible previous law-study coursework and documented supervision hours, subject to state requirements. |
| High School Grades 9–12 | Based on completed credits | Transfer credits evaluated according to the student's grade level and completed coursework. Eligible high school courses may also be satisfied through **RIAH-approved alternative course equivalents** where accepted for the applicable grade-level and state graduation requirements. |
| High School Entering After Grade 8 | Based on applicable prior coursework | Complete approved Grades 9–12 diploma requirements through Regular or Dual Enrollment; eligible secondary courses may use approved equivalents. **Dual Enrollment** adds concurrent college General Education and applicable School Core courses to the high school diploma pathway; eligible college courses may also be satisfied through RIAH-approved alternative credits or evaluated prior college coursework. |
| GED/HSE | No transfer into the four required preparation courses | Four preparation courses, applicable state-specific requirements, and optional concurrent General Education credit. |
| Experiential | Program-specific | **Two separate eligibility requirements, evaluated by the selected major and Experiential level (Apprentice through Executive): (1) the applicable major-specific prerequisite coursework and (2) verified prior experience qualifying for that level.** Required prerequisites may be completed through RIAH's own curriculum or satisfied through RIAH-approved equivalent alternative coursework (including Sophia.org where an equivalent is approved) or approved transfer coursework from another institution. For example, **Computer Science requires Principles of Computer Science**; Accounting requires **Financial Accounting AND Managerial Accounting**, as established in the Experiential Structure. **Apprentice:** entry level with little to no prior coursework or experience; **Intern:** some applicable foundational coursework and little to no professional experience; **Associate:** required major prerequisites and some applicable experience; **Senior Associate:** required prerequisites and at least one year of applicable experience; **Manager:** required prerequisites and Senior Associate-level experience; **Executive:** required prerequisites and at least one year of Manager-level experience. RIAH reviews both coursework and experience for the selected level; alternative/transfer coursework cannot replace the experience requirement. Placement remains subject to eligibility verification, supervision, and capacity under `RIAH-PATHWAY-EXPERIENTIAL-STRUCTURE/EXPERIENTIAL-STRUCTURE.md`. |

# VI. ALEKS AND RIAH PLACEMENT ASSESSMENTS

## DEMONSTRATE YOUR KNOWLEDGE.

RIAH uses placement assessments to determine approved academic placement and eligible course credit.

### Placement Assessment Requirements

| Component | Requirement |
|---|---|
| Mathematics Placement | ALEKS. |
| Passing Standard | **80% or higher.** |
| General Education Placement | RIAH-approved subject assessment. |
| Maximum Attempts | 3. |
| Waiting Period | Minimum 24 hours between attempts. |
| Proctoring | Required under RIAH's placement-assessment policy. |
| Successful Assessment | Applicable requirement satisfied under approved RIAH credit policy. |
| Unsuccessful Attempt | No academic penalty. |
| Three Unsuccessful Attempts | Complete the applicable RIAH course. |
| College English | Placement determines the applicable English entry level. |
| College Algebra | ALEKS mathematics placement determines the applicable mathematics entry level. |
| Developmental Coursework | Basic English or Basic Mathematics where required. |
| Academic Progression | Complete the required course or approved placement before proceeding. |
