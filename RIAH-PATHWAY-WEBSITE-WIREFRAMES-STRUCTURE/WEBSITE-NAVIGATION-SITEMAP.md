### 7.1 — STANDARD HIGH SCHOOL PARTNERSHIP TRACK

RIAH Pathway partners with high schools throughout all 50 states and Washington, D.C. to offer eligible students experiential pathways (Apprentice–Executive), entrepreneurship programs, certification review, applicable bar review, and educational pathways from minors through J.D., subject to each program's admission and eligibility requirements. The Standard Track uses the selected category's pricing; the two high-school dual-enrollment tracks remain General Education Only and First Bachelor's Degree. Other educational pathways, including minors, associate's, master's, MBA and J.D., use their respective applications. High school partners may also refer students who have left high school to RIAH Pathway's GED/HSE preparation programs, subject to GED/HSE eligibility. RIAH Pathway does not issue high school diplomas; partner high schools remain responsible for their diplomas.
