Lead cybersecurity, network and infrastructure security, access management, risk management, monitoring, vulnerability management, and incident response throughout the RIAH Pathway education, technology, and experiential ecosystem.

## II. 🔐 KEY RESPONSIBILITIES

| # | Responsibility / Expectation |
|---:|---|
| 1 | Recruit, hire, supervise, and delegate work to Red, Blue, and Purple Team Specialists. |
| 2 | Establish policies, encryption, access controls, monitoring, automation, incident response, and vulnerability management. |
| 3 | Secure applications, databases, servers, endpoints, websites, networks, backups, proprietary systems, and communications. |
| 4 | Protect the Founder/CEO's approved laptop, mobile phone, hotspot, internet connectivity, and private communications. |
| 5 | Coordinate configurations, authentication, updates, and authorized security and technology access with the CTO. |
| 6 | Protect the Founder/CEO's laptop, phone, hotspot, internet connectivity, phone service and private communications while leading the Red, Blue and Purple Team Specialists. |
