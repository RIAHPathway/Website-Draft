## I. 🛡️ ABOUT THE POSITION

RIAH Pathway is seeking an experienced **Chief Information Security Officer (CISO)** to lead cybersecurity, infrastructure protection, network security, identity and access management, risk management, monitoring, and incident response across our education, technology, and experiential ecosystem.

The CISO works directly with the Founder/CEO, CTO, and Backend Technology Team while recruiting and managing the **Red, Blue, and Purple Team Specialists**.

## II. 🔐 KEY RESPONSIBILITIES

| # | Responsibility | Description |
|---:|---|---|
| 1 | Cybersecurity Leadership | Recruit, hire, supervise, and delegate responsibilities to Red, Blue, and Purple Team Specialists. |
| 2 | Security Architecture | Establish policies, encryption, access controls, monitoring, automation, incident response, and vulnerability management. |
| 3 | Infrastructure Protection | Secure applications, databases, servers, networks, websites, endpoints, proprietary systems, and communications. |
| 4 | Founder/CEO Protection | Protect the Founder/CEO's laptop, mobile phone, hotspot, internet connectivity, phone service, and private communications. |
| 5 | Technology Coordination | Work with the CTO on configurations, authentication, backups, updates, authorized access, and cybersecurity workflows. |
| 6 | Team Development | Oversee cybersecurity operations while executive, faculty, and operational hiring continues. |

## III. 🎓 QUALIFICATIONS & EXPERIENCE

| # | Category | Requirements |

| 2 | Preferred | Security architecture, network security, infrastructure protection, risk management, incident response, and security operations. |
