## Equity Contribution Pool Requirement

All individuals receiving **RIAH Pathway equity** are required to contribute to the RIAH Pathway **Contribution Pool**. Contributions are due on the **15th of each month**.

The Contribution Pool supports shared organizational expenses, including **advertising, marketing, legal expenses, accreditation, authorization, business filings, technology, operational expenses, and other approved RIAH Pathway organizational costs**.

The contribution amount is based on the approved annual Contribution Pool budget for that stage of RIAH Pathway's growth and the number of equity-bearing individuals participating in the pool.

### Pre-Beta Contribution Pool — 48 Contributors

RIAH Pathway begins with a **48-person pre-beta equity-bearing team**. These 48 individuals represent the fixed internal team members being hired before the 120 core Experiential Professionals.

The 48-person pre-beta team is responsible for helping RIAH Pathway prepare for beta operations, including the work required across **marketing, advertising, legal, accreditation, authorization, technology, operations, academic preparation, curriculum, products, systems, infrastructure, and organizational development**.

The projected annual pre-beta Contribution Pool budget is approximately **$300,000**.

| Pre-Beta Contribution Calculation | Amount |
|---|---:|
| Annual Contribution Pool Budget | **$300,000** |
| Equity-Bearing Contributors | **48** |
| Annual Contribution Per Person | **$6,250.00** |
| Monthly Contribution Per Person | **$520.83** |
| Contribution Due Date | **15th of Each Month** |

**$300,000 ÷ 48 = $6,250.00 annually per person**

**$6,250.00 ÷ 12 = $520.83 monthly per person**

### Beta Experiential Hiring Expansion

After the pre-beta team completes the preparation required for RIAH Pathway to move into beta, RIAH Pathway begins hiring the **120 core Experiential Professionals**.

The Beta stage grows from the original **48 pre-beta equity-bearing individuals** toward the full **168-person fixed internal equity-bearing team** as Experiential Professionals are hired.

| Growth Stage | Equity-Bearing People |
|---|---:|
| Pre-Beta Team | **48** |
| Core Experiential Professionals Available for Beta Hiring | **Up to 120** |
| **TOTAL FIXED INTERNAL TEAM AT SCALE** | **168** |

The 120 core Experiential Professionals become part of the fixed internal equity-bearing team as they are hired and participate in the Contribution Pool.

During Beta, both the number of contributors and the annual Contribution Pool budget may change as Experiential hiring progresses toward the 168-person at-scale structure.

Once the 120 core Experiential Professional positions have been filled, additional Experiential Professionals required because of student demand are hired as **external contractors**. Those additional contractors do not receive equity and do not participate in the equity Contribution Pool.

### At-Scale Contribution Pool — 168 Contributors

At scale, the fixed internal equity-bearing team consists of **168 individuals**, including the initial 48 team members and the 120 core Experiential Professionals.

The projected annual Contribution Pool budget at scale is **$1,200,000**.

# II. RIAH PATHWAY AT-SCALE STAFFING STRUCTURE

RIAH Pathway's **core fixed internal team is 168 people**. Within that 168-person core, **120 are core Experiential Professionals**. The 120-person Experiential structure represents the equity-bearing Experiential team at scale. When student enrollment and student demand require additional Experiential Professionals, RIAH Pathway will hire additional professionals according to that demand. Those additional demand-based hires increase the operational Experiential workforce but **do not increase the 168-person equity-bearing core team and do not receive equity under the 20% fixed internal team allocation**.

RIAH Pathway also maintains an **external demand-based contractor team** for services offered through the RIAH Pathway product ecosystem. These external contractors may support **mentorship, coaching, live reviews, Certification Review services, Bar Review services, academic support, professional support, and other service-based products offered through the website and RIAH Pathway ecosystem**. These professionals are hired according to product demand, student demand, sales volume, service utilization, and operational need. They receive **contract compensation based on the services and products they support and do not receive organizational equity**.

All RIAH Pathway team roles operate **100% remotely**, except for required in-person attendance by the **Executive Leadership Team and Board of Directors** for quarterly Board meetings in **Cleveland, Ohio**. Executive Leadership may also be required to attend in-person **marketing, public, community, organizational, partnership, accreditation, graduation, and other significant RIAH Pathway events**.

These supervisors support students through the law curriculum, practical legal training, supervised legal work, state requirements, and required documentation and review. The attorney and judge supervisors are **not included within the fixed 168-person internal team**.

The JD program does not use a separate fixed student-capacity limit for supervision within the RIAH Pathway structure. # XXXII. COMPENSATION, BENEFITS, REMOTE STRUCTURE & AT-SCALE TRANSITION

RIAH Pathway begins with an **equity + contract + revenue-split compensation structure** while the ecosystem is in its startup, pre-beta, beta, accreditation, authorization, and growth stages.

Once RIAH Pathway reaches **at scale, post-accreditation, and Title IV operations**, the **168-person fixed internal team** transitions into full-time employment with **100% benefits provided by RIAH Pathway**, including **medical / health, dental, vision, and standard employee and team benefits and perks**.

RIAH Pathway operates as a **100% remote organization for its regular team positions**, except for the required in-person responsibilities of the Executive Leadership Team and Board of Directors.

The **external team** remains contract-based. This includes additional Experiential Professionals and product-service professionals hired for mentorship, coaching, live review, Certification Review, Bar Review, and other product services.

# XXXIII. BOARD OF DIRECTORS — NON-EQUITY

The RIAH Pathway Board of Directors is separate from the **168-person fixed internal equity-bearing team**, the external contractor workforce, and the JD / Non-JD attorney and judge supervision structure.

The Board conducts **quarterly in-person Board meetings in Cleveland, Ohio**. Board members are required to attend these quarterly meetings in person. The five members of the Executive Leadership Team are also required to attend the quarterly Board meetings in person with the Board.

Each Board member receives a **$5,000 annual Board meeting stipend**, divided across the four quarterly meetings. This equals **$1,250 per quarter per Board member** for **lodging, food, and transportation**.
